I Commit!

Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

I Commit! is a personal commitment tracker made and run by Cathy Zhang. This page explains what the app stores, why, who else handles it, and how you can delete it. In short: your data is used only to run the app for you. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and the app has no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (commitments, ticks and logged time, notes, knowledge trees, test results) is kept in your browser's storage on each device. So are the calendar (iCal) links you add and your background picture. If you never sign in, nothing else leaves your device, except as described under "Calendar links" below.

What the server stores, and why

The app's server runs on Cloudflare Workers and stores data in a Cloudflare D1 database. It stores only what the features you turn on need:

DataWhy
Your Google account ID, email address and nameTo know which account is yours when you sign in with Google
A synced copy of your app data, filed under a hash of a random keyTo keep your devices the same
A plan of your next 8 days: item names, dates and times, and which routines are tickedTo send reminders and the morning summary while the app is closed
Your reminder settings and, for each device with notifications on, its push address, encryption keys and type (e.g. "iPhone")To send push notifications to your devices
Google access tokens for Calendar and Gmail, and a list linking each copied item to its Google Calendar eventTo keep the I Commit! calendar up to date and send your summary email
Your Moodle security key, if you connect MoodleTo show your courses in Academics
A short summary of today's progress, if you turn on the phone widgetSo the widget can show it

Work Buddy, calendar sharing, Gallery projects and Academics choices are stored in a Supabase database. Its access rules let only you, and the people you choose to share with, see your rows.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, or connect Google Calendar and Gmail, the app asks for these permissions and uses them only as follows:

  • Sign in (openid, email, profile): your Google account ID, email address and name, to identify your account and to show who is signed in.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.app.created): to make one calendar called "I Commit!" and to add, change and delete events in that calendar only. The app cannot see or change your other calendars.
  • Gmail (gmail.send): to send one summary email a day from your Gmail to your own address. The app cannot read, search or delete your email.

I Commit!'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not transferred to anyone else, except as needed to run these features, for security, or where the law requires it. No person reads it, unless you ask for help with your data, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

Who else handles your data

  • Cloudflare: hosts the app and its database.
  • Supabase: hosts the Work Buddy, sharing, Gallery and Academics database.
  • Google: sign-in, and Google Calendar and Gmail if you connect them.
  • Push services (Apple, Google or Mozilla, depending on your device): deliver notifications. Each notification is encrypted for your device, so the push service can't read it.
  • Moodle (LSE): only if you connect it; the app only reads your courses.
  • Notion: the Applications tab reads one application tracker that the app's owner set up.

Calendar links

When you add an iCal link, the server fetches the calendar for your device and passes it straight back. It doesn't store the link or the calendar.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Google access: in the app, open ⋯ → Reminders & Google Calendar → Disconnect. This revokes the app's Google access. You can also remove it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The I Commit! calendar stays in your Google Calendar until you delete it there.
  • Notifications: turn them off on the same page. A device is also forgotten automatically when its browser drops the subscription.
  • Synced data: "Delete the synced copy" removes it from the server. Deleted items are remembered as deleted for up to 180 days, so a device that was offline doesn't bring them back.
  • Reminder records: the list of reminders already sent is kept for 3 days.
  • Everything else: to delete your whole account and all its data, ask on the project's GitHub page. It will be deleted within 30 days.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Your sync key is stored only as a hash, Google and Moodle tokens stay on the server and are never sent to your browser, and push notifications are encrypted end to end.

Children

I Commit! is not meant for children under 13.
