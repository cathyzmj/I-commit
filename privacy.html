I Commit!

Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

I Commit! is a personal commitment tracker made and run by Cathy Zhang. This page explains what the app stores, why, who else handles it, and how you can delete it. In short: your data is used only to run the app for you. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and the app has no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (commitments, ticks and logged time, notes, knowledge trees, test results) is kept in your browser's storage on each device. So are the calendar (iCal) links you add and your background picture. If you never sign in, nothing else leaves your device, except as described under "Calendar links" below.

What the server stores, and why

The app's server runs on Cloudflare Workers and stores data in a Cloudflare D1 database. It stores only what the features you turn on need:

DataWhy
Your Google account ID, email address and nameTo know which account is yours when you sign in with Google
A synced copy of your app data, filed under a hash of a random keyTo keep your devices the same
A plan of your next 8 days: item names, dates and times, and which routines are tickedTo send reminders and the morning briefing while the app is closed
Your reminder settings, and the ID and name of the Telegram chat you connectTo send your reminders to that chat
The items in your calendar link (timed one-offs, project meetings, application dates), if you make oneSo Google Calendar, or another calendar app, can read the link
Your Moodle security key, if you connect MoodleTo show your courses in Academics
A short summary of today's progress, if you turn on the phone widgetSo the widget can show it

Work Buddy, calendar sharing, Gallery projects and Academics choices are stored in a Supabase database. Its access rules let only you, and the people you choose to share with, see your rows.

Google user data

The app uses Google only to sign you in. It asks for your Google account ID, email address and name (the openid, email and profile permissions), to identify your account and to show who is signed in. It has no access to your Google Calendar, Gmail, Drive or anything else in your Google account.

If you add the calendar link to Google Calendar, Google fetches the link like any website; the app doesn't sign in to your calendar to do this.

I Commit!'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to sign you in. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not transferred to anyone else, except as needed to run these features, for security, or where the law requires it. No person reads it, unless you ask for help with your data, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

Who else handles your data

  • Cloudflare: hosts the app and its database.
  • Supabase: hosts the Work Buddy, sharing, Gallery and Academics database.
  • Google: sign-in, and Google Calendar reads your calendar link if you add it there.
  • Telegram: delivers your reminders, if you connect it. Telegram sees the reminder text, like any message, under its own privacy policy.
  • Moodle (LSE): only if you connect it; the app only reads your courses.
  • Notion: the Applications tab reads one application tracker that the app's owner set up.

Calendar links

When you add an iCal link, the server fetches the calendar for your device and passes it straight back. It doesn't store the link or the calendar.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Telegram: in the app, open ⋯ → Reminders & Google Calendar → Disconnect, or send the bot /stop. Blocking the bot also disconnects it.
  • Calendar link: "Stop sharing" on the same page deletes the link and its items; "Make a new link" stops the old one. Remove the I Commit! calendar from Google Calendar too.
  • Synced data: "Delete the synced copy" removes it from the server. Deleted items are remembered as deleted for up to 180 days, so a device that was offline doesn't bring them back.
  • Reminder records: the list of reminders already sent is kept for 3 days.
  • Everything else: to delete your whole account and all its data, ask on the project's GitHub page. It will be deleted within 30 days.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Your sync key is stored only as a hash, and Moodle keys stay on the server and are never sent to your browser. Your calendar link contains a long random code; anyone who has the link can read it, so keep it private, and make a new one if it leaks.

Children

I Commit! is not meant for children under 13.
